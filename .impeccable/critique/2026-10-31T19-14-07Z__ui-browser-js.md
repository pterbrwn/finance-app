## Design specificity

Ledger’s quiet green palette, financial figures, and clear sample label suit a household money tool. Its strongest character is the distinction between actual, planned, and hypothetical money. The repeated card pattern remains fairly interchangeable, and ownership and safe-editing gaps weaken the careful tone. The bundled detector found one advisory warning about Inter at `ui/styles.css:1`, zero errors, and did not catch the workflow issues. No reliable user-visible detector overlay was established.

## Overall impression

Review before bulk categorization and shared Escape handling improve trust. A cancel path still loses drafts, and month changes can carry selections into a new result scope. The next pass should finish safety behavior, then reduce mobile scrolling to the primary answer.

## Priority issues

1. **P1 — Cancel silently discards a draft.** The shared guard covers `[data-close]` and `[data-cancel]`, but `#cancel-import` calls `closeDialog` directly (`ui/dialogs.mjs:16`, `ui/browser.js:321`). Chromium confirmed that an edited account field closes with no prompt. Route all dismissal controls through one dirty-form decision and audit other `#cancel-*` handlers. Suggested command: `$impeccable harden`.
2. **P1 — Month changes retain selected transactions.** The month handler does not clear selected IDs or review confirmation (`ui/browser.js:294`). An old-month selection can remain in the new month’s bulk controls. Clear and announce selection when month scope changes; require fresh review. Suggested command: `$impeccable harden`.
3. **P1 — Joint budget editing has a misleading identity cue.** Combined Budgets offers Start Joint plan, but the global selector lacks Joint; Household can remain shown as edits target Joint (`ui/browser.js:156,268`). Show the actual edit owner at the action and in the editor, or make Joint selectable. Suggested command: `$impeccable clarify`.
4. **P2 — The early scenario estimate conflicts with saving.** Four core inputs yield a rough payment, while Save scenario requires financed fees and collapsed cost fields, with PMI also required (`ui/browser.js:226`). Label the early result as preview, reveal save requirements before the action, and allow partial drafts if the product contract supports them. Suggested commands: `$impeccable onboard`, `$impeccable clarify`.
5. **P2 — Mobile pages bury the everyday answer.** At 390px, the first transaction begins around y=918, below an 844px viewport. Budgets puts planned surplus after ten repeated bucket cards (`ui/browser.js:152,169`). Put results and a compact month summary first; group secondary filters and details. Suggested commands: `$impeccable layout`, `$impeccable adapt`.

## Personas, cognitive load, and emotional journey

- First-time partner: reassuring Overview, but Joint/Household labeling and late scenario requirements invite misunderstanding.
- Power user: a genuine bulk review step now exists, but month switching can invalidate selection context.
- Distracted mobile user: transaction results and budget surplus require unnecessary scrolling.
- Keyboard and low-vision user: empty-view axe pass is encouraging, but populated states and screen-reader use remain unverified.

Cognitive load is moderate: mobile task focus, minimal choices, and progressive disclosure fail. The trust peak is the new review step; the valley is losing an import draft after Cancel.
